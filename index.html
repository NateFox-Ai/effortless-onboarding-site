Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the free audit and a paid plan?

The free audit is a no-commitment 30-minute call to explore what's possible and build you a roadmap. A paid plan means we actually build the automation for you. Both paths start with a 1-on-1 call — the difference is with a paid plan, that call is your kickoff and we get to work immediately after.

If I pay, when do I book my kickoff call?

Right after checkout. You'll be redirected to a scheduling page to pick a time that works for you. Most clients are booked within 24–48 hours of payment.

What tools do you use for automation?

We primarily use n8n, Make.com, and custom integrations depending on your needs. We work with your existing tools — Calendly, Stripe, CRMs, email platforms, etc.

How long does setup take?

Most automation setups take 1-2 weeks from kickoff to launch. Complex multi-step workflows may take 3-4 weeks.

Do I need technical knowledge?

Nope! We handle all the technical work. You just need to know your workflow — we'll translate that into automation.

What if I need changes later?

That's what our maintenance plan is for. Or you can request one-off changes at our hourly rate ($150/hr).

Is my data secure?

We use established tools like n8n, Make.com, and your existing platforms (Stripe, Calendly, etc.) — all of which have strong security practices. Your data stays in your accounts; we just connect the dots.
